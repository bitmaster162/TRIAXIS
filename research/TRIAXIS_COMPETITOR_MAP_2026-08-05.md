# TRIAXIS Competitor Map — 2026-08-05

## Market structure

TRIAXIS does not face one exact competitor. It overlaps four product categories.

### 1. Runtime identity and authorization

- Google Cloud Agent Identity, Agent Gateway, IAP/CAA for agents, Principal Access Boundaries, Model Armor.
- AIRGuard, SafeAgent and runtime-governance research prototypes.
- AgentShield-style runtime firewalls.

Their advantage: placement at the real tool/resource boundary, enterprise identity and runtime enforcement.

TRIAXIS gap: no production identity provider, KMS, gateway or multi-host enforcement.

TRIAXIS possible differentiation: the authorization request is derived from a typed decision assurance case with preserved defeaters and falsification evidence, rather than only from the proposed tool call.

### 2. Agent evaluation and release governance

- ProofAgent: adversarial evaluation, governance-as-code, release gates, compliance evidence and continuous assurance.
- Galileo: trajectory observability, evaluation and runtime guardrails.
- Patronus AI: agent trajectory evaluation, simulation and issue localization.
- Guardrails AI / Snowglobe: validators, simulation, replay and agent profiles.

Their advantage: usable platforms, CI/CD integration, dashboards and customer traction.

TRIAXIS gap: no polished evaluation product, integrations, dashboards or independent customer evidence.

TRIAXIS possible differentiation: explicit decision-case semantics, authority monotonicity and decision-to-action subject binding.

### 3. Policy and provenance building blocks

- OPA/policy-as-code, Zero Trust, SPIFFE/SPIRE.
- in-toto, TUF, Sigstore and tamper-evident supply-chain attestations.
- GSN/SACM assurance cases.

Their advantage: mature deterministic and cryptographic mechanisms.

TRIAXIS must reuse these patterns rather than reimplement them as LLM prose.

### 4. Cognitive oversight research

- Constitutional AI and deliberative alignment.
- AI Safety via Debate and scalable oversight.
- Self-Refine, Reflexion and verifier-driven refinement.
- Process supervision and external verifiers.

Their advantage: prior research and some empirical evidence.

TRIAXIS gap: no independent empirical proof that Devil/Angel/Synthesizer improve outcomes.

## Positioning decision

Do not position TRIAXIS as a new alignment theory or a generic guardrail product.

Preferred category:

> Decision Assurance and Action Authorization for high-consequence AI workflows.

Wedge products:

1. AI-Agent Reliability Audit.
2. Research Assurance / multi-model adjudication.
3. Decision Assurance Engine for operator decisions.
4. Execution Firewall integration after evidence from the first three.

## Competitive moat hypothesis

The moat cannot be role prompts. It must be accumulated assets:

- domain-specific failure corpus;
- calibrated falsification contracts;
- evidence and defeater graph;
- policy packs;
- signed decision/execution receipts;
- production incident and regression memory;
- connectors to real systems;
- independent benchmark results.
