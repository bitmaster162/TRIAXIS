# TRIAXIS Research and Product Roadmap

## Product verdict

TRIAXIS is a strong integrated governance hypothesis and a useful audit framework. It is not yet proven as a superior reasoning architecture and should not be marketed as a new alignment theory.

## Commercial sequence

### Phase 1 — service wedge

- AI-Agent Reliability Audit.
- Multi-model Research Assurance.
- 7-Day Operator Decision Sprint with a Decision Assurance Receipt.

Goal: collect real failure cases, false positives, human review time and willingness to pay.

### Phase 2 — reusable product

- Evidence Broker.
- Decision Assurance Case compiler.
- Falsification Contract builder.
- Human Decision Cockpit.
- CI/CD evaluation and release gate.

### Phase 3 — runtime control

- Agent identity and task-bound credentials.
- Policy-as-code adapter.
- Tool/resource gateway.
- Single-use action token and durable receipt.
- Unknown-outcome reconciliation.

### Phase 4 — platform and protocol

- Domain policy packs.
- Open-source SDK and schemas.
- Public FAIL-BENCH and model-role leaderboard.
- Enterprise hosted/on-prem control plane.
- Decision Assurance Receipt interoperability proposal.

## Highest-information research

1. Equal-budget ablation: single strong model versus same-model roles versus heterogeneous branches versus external verifier plus gate.
2. Correlated blind-spot injection.
3. Semantic substitution of destructive actions.
4. Source-correlation and citation-entailment attacks.
5. Blind review versus full-context review.
6. Falsifier quality: measurable tests, false discriminators and decision-update compliance.
7. Devil precision/recall and objection flooding.
8. Angel effect on over-refusal and unsafe pass rate.
9. Synthesizer bias and minority-defeater preservation.
10. Assumption injection and context-gap preservation.
11. Stale policy, stale approval and stale state.
12. Runtime path-dependent policy versus static access control.
13. Human escalation quality and review time.
14. Cost/latency versus legitimate value preserved.
15. Independent reproduction by another implementation and evaluator.

## Metrics

- task success and correctness;
- unsafe action rate;
- false denial and over-refusal;
- critical contradiction recall/precision;
- hallucination persistence;
- calibration and false confidence;
- evidence independence and freshness;
- reviewer true/false defect rate;
- escalation quality and human time;
- latency, tokens and monetary cost;
- reproducibility and provenance integrity;
- Net Governance Utility.

## Falsification condition for the project

TRIAXIS should be simplified or rejected if, at equal total compute and human-review budget, it does not materially outperform `Proposer + external verifier + deterministic gate` on unsafe-action reduction, defect discovery and preserved legitimate utility.
