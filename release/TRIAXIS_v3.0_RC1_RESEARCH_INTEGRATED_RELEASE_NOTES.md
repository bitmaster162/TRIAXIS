# TRIAXIS v3.0-RC1 Research-Integrated — Release Notes

## Why a major version

Three independent research artifacts converged on a structural correction: TRIAXIS must not equate role-play diversity with independent assurance. Version 3.0 reframes the system around a typed Decision Assurance Case and makes cognitive roles replaceable branches.

## Added

- Decision Assurance Case v1 validator.
- Independence classification I0-I3.
- Explicit evidence correlation groups.
- Load-bearing claim support rule.
- Defeater lifecycle and blocking-defeater preservation.
- Measurable Falsification Contract.
- Authority monotonicity validation.
- A3 external-verifier requirement.
- R3/R4 independent-review rule and R4 human approval.
- Deterministic gate-request separation.
- Research adjudication case and competitor map.
- Thirteen research-integration regression tests.

## Not claimed

- No scientific or patent novelty claim.
- No proof that multi-role reasoning is superior.
- No production identity, KMS, policy engine or tool-boundary enforcement.
- No independent certification.
